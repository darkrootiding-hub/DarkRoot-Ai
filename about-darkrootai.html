Get started

Using DarkRoot AI

No account required to start. Three steps from opening the app to your first real answer.

01

Open the app and just ask

Type a question the way you'd ask a person. There's no mode to pick first — DarkRoot AI reads the request and routes it to the right model on its own.

02

Switch modes for specific work

Building a website or a game? Switch to Web Dev or Game Dev mode from the bar above the message box for a live preview panel alongside the code.

03

Bring your own key, if you want

Add a personal API key in Settings and your requests go straight to the provider from your device, bypassing DarkRoot's servers entirely. Optional — most people never need to.
